## Reconciliation of published values

- **Raw versus corrected:** Ascella Fig. S1 explicitly distinguishes raw HOM visibility from corrected M_s. The plan's `.93` is a rounding, while `.9438` is the corrected quantity; they are not conflicting measurements of an identically defined parameter. The notebook confirms use of the latter central value with the same g² central value, not a new measurement. [Ascella supplement][supp]; [tomography notebook][tomo].
- **Rounded versus detailed loss:** approximately 8% in the main paper and 8.4% ± 0.2% in its loss-budget table are respectively summary and detailed reports. [Main paper][ascella]; [supplement][supp].
- **Source boundaries and dates:** first-lens brightness, filtered fibre brightness, and complete source-to-detection efficiency refer to different boundaries. The 2024 and Q1 2026 standalone product specifications also have different brightness and guaranteed indistinguishability values; neither establishes the fitted parameters of every deployed cloud QPU. [2024 catalogue][cat24]; [Q1 2026 catalogue][cat26].
- **Different detector configurations:** the shadow paper's Ascella experiment explicitly uses threshold detectors. Its Appendix D additionally describes an unnamed mixed configuration (12 restricted-PNR plus 12 threshold), whereas the Belenos reservoir paper explicitly identifies PNR capability on 16 output modes. A configuration change or a shared implementation is not verified; neither passage proves unlimited number resolution. [Shadow paper][shadows]; [reservoir paper][qrp].
- **Presentation versus experiments:** the company slide's generic threshold wording and >95% detection statement differ from the specifically measured Ascella efficiency and the later paper's Belenos PNR description. The slide does not identify a machine, measurement date, or system-efficiency boundary; it cannot reconcile those configurations by itself. [Company presentation][slides]; [Ascella supplement][supp]; [reservoir paper][qrp].

## Not found / could not verify

- **Altair stop condition:** after searches of Quandela technical/product material, Perceval remote documentation and 1.2.4 client code, and primary papers for named platforms, no primary source tying Altair specifically to threshold, PNR, or pseudo-PNR detection was found. Its classification remains **not found**; no inference from general SNSPD literature is made.
- No verified detector dead time for Ascella, Altair, or the Belenos/MosaiQ configurations. Ascella's coincidence window is not dead time. No verified detector efficiency/dark-count calibration or end-to-end transmission for Belenos or Altair in the inspected papers.
- No verified statement that a single Ascella physical output detector resolves two simultaneously incident photons. Its published pseudo-PNR protocol uses additional modes. The Belenos reservoir paper states PNR capability but leaves maximum number, efficiency versus occupation, and physical readout mechanism unspecified in the inspected Methods passage.
- No evidence identifying the shadow paper's unnamed mixed-detector appendix configuration as Belenos. No verified correspondence between that configuration and the reservoir paper's 16 PNR-capable modes.
- No authenticated live cloud inventory, detector architecture, or performance snapshot; no sampling jobs submitted. Client metadata handling is verified, current server configuration is not. The installed package metadata reports `perceval-quandela 1.2.4`; installed relevant source and public tagged source were inspected separately. Public versioned code is the cited evidence.
- Searches included `Ascella detectors efficiency`, `Quandela Altair threshold`, `Quandela Belenos detectors`, `Ascella pseudo detectors`, and official-domain queries for Altair photon-detector specifications; followed paper supplements, catalogue links, tomography and detector references, and versioned source. Secondary articles were used only as discovery leads, not as evidence for values.
- **Access:** Nature article and exact supplementary PDF were read; catalogues and public presentation were read by direct PDF retrieval when the web reader failed. No paywalled-only source was used. The first guessed supplementary URL returned 404; the exact publisher link above succeeded. Guessed Altair product URLs and Perceval v1.2 RemoteProcessor reference/module pages were inaccessible to the web reader; they remain **unread**, without a verified paywall explanation. Public tagged client source and the accessible remote tutorial were used instead.

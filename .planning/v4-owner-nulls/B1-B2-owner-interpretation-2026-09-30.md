# OWNER INTERPRETATION (B1/B2, one fit per profile/n; no intervals)

Date: 2026-09-30

B1: The odd-order residuals in B1 are much smaller than the even-order ones because the
rings target is nearly unchanged under flipping all bits, and for odd |S| that flip
changes the sign, so the target's odd moments are near 0. Since the residual is also
small there, the model's odd moments are near 0 too. At orders 6, 7 and 8 both fits
produce nothing (their residual equals the target's own energy). My guess is that the
model doesn't reproduce the rings well, and B1 says the shortfall is mostly at order 4,
which holds about 60% of the target's energy.

B2: Keeping the low orders beats random subsets at L = 2 but not at L = 1, because
odd-order moments add nothing to the rebuilt distribution since they cancel out
(parity) and even-order moments do add to the rebuilt distribution.

  <decision>
Two mechanism details inside the already-locked pooled/recycled scheme, both of which the Forge model is about to encode as premises:

(a) The pooling-compatibility rule: two pairs `(i,j)` and `(i',j')` may share an ancilla block if and only if `{i,j} ∩ {i',j'} = ∅` (vertex-disjointness).

(b) The concretization of "pooled/recycled": a FIXED, subset-independent round-robin edge-colouring of `K_n` — `colour(i,j) = (i+j) mod n` for odd `n` using `K = n` blocks; for even `n`, with `m = n-1`, `colour(i,j) = (i+j) mod m` for `i,j < m` and `colour(i, n-1) = (2i) mod m`, using `K = n-1` blocks.
  </decision>
  <context>
Why this is being asked rather than decided silently: `22-CONTEXT.md` puts the compatibility rule under Claude's Discretion but explicitly instructs that it be stated precisely and flagged back, since it is the one piece of the scheme's mechanics the owner has not itself reviewed. `22-RESEARCH.md` Open Question 1 adds that D-02 locked the category, not a specific formula, and that the owner's mental model may have been closer to a dynamic/greedy scheme.

What rides on (b): the fixed-vs-dynamic choice is not cosmetic. It determines whether the phase is tractable at all. A fixed, subset-independent allocation is what licenses the pairwise-reduction argument (collision is a binary predicate, so subset-quantification collapses to `C(28,2)+28 = 406` pairwise cases at `n=8`). A dynamic/greedy allocation recomputed per active set breaks that reduction, reintroduces the alternating-quantifier shape that this environment's own `prim.frg` example annotates as "takes longer to run than I am willing to wait" even at a five-node bound, and would make the `2^28` framing genuinely load-bearing rather than reducible.

What (b) does NOT claim: the chromatic index of `K_n` is a known theorem (`n-1` for even `n`, `n` for odd `n`, Koenig/Vizing). This formula constructively realizes settled combinatorics. Choosing it does not manufacture novelty, and the write-up will say so plainly.

Note also that the Forge model will NOT encode this formula. Per D-05 it searches freely over block assignments for a minimum `K`; the formula is the independently-constructed witness whose colour count must agree with what Forge finds. So confirming (b) fixes the specification and the baseline, not the model's search space.
  </context>
